/ About

Two careers. One brain.

I ship product by day and write hits by night. Sometimes the other way around.

I'm a software engineer from St. John's, Newfoundland, with a Computer Science degree from Memorial University. I like being early: I was a founding engineer and first hire, a startup co-founder and CEO, one of the first 10 engineers at a legal-AI company now valued at $350M, and an early developer at a startup acquired within two years of founding. I'm currently building at Melotech, which works at the intersection of AI, media and entertainment.

In music, I'm a topliner, producer and vocalist signed with Seoul's Melogram, and I co-founded the production company Gilkicker Music. My songs have landed with &TEAM, THE BOYZ, ALL(H)OURS and SUPERKIND, and my voice is on WARFRAME's in-game boy band On-lyne.

“I love making music . . . but I wouldn't be where I am in startups right now if I wasn't passionate about it.”

Jacob Critch, interviewed by Entrevestor (Dec 2025)
